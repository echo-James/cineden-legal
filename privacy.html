CineDen for Plex

Privacy Policy

Effective 10 September 2026 Version 1.0

CineDen is a video player for Meta Quest that connects to your own Plex server. It has no account system, no server of its own, and no analytics. This policy describes the only data it touches, all of which either stays on your headset or goes to services you already use.

The short version

CineDen sends nothing about you to us, because there is no “us” to send it to. We operate no servers and collect no data. Your Plex sign-in stays on your headset. Your viewing goes directly between your headset and your own Plex server. We never see your library, your history, or your account.

What stays on your headset

CineDen saves a small amount of information in its own private storage area on the headset, which other apps on the device cannot read. None of it is transmitted to us. It exists so the app remembers your setup between sessions.

Everything CineDen writes to the device
Stored as What it holds Why
AuthToken The access token your Plex server issued to this app So you do not sign in again on every launch
AccountToken The access token for your Plex account To list your servers and Plex Home profiles
ClientIdentifier A random identifier the app generates on first run Plex ties your sign-in to it, so it must stay stable
SelectedServer The address and name of the server you chose To reconnect to the same server next time
ActiveHomeUser Which Plex Home profile you picked To open in the right profile
Settings Screen size, distance, curvature, colours, comfort options To keep the room the way you arranged it
ControlsGuideSeen Whether you have seen the controls guide So it is shown once rather than every launch

The random identifier is generated on your device and is not derived from anything about you or your hardware. It is not shared with anyone except your own Plex server and the Plex sign-in service, which requires it.

What leaves your headset, and where it goes

CineDen talks to exactly two kinds of destination. Both are necessary for the app to function, and neither is operated by us.

plex.tv

Plex's own service, used to sign you in, to discover which servers your account can reach, and to list your Plex Home profiles. Sign-in uses Plex's code-linking flow: the app shows you a short code, and you enter it at plex.tv/link in your own browser. CineDen never sees or handles your Plex password.

Each request identifies the app with:

  • app name and version
  • the random client identifier
  • platform (“Android”)
  • operating system version
  • device type (“Meta Quest”)
  • your headset's device name
  • your Plex token, once signed in

Plex requires these so the app appears in the authorised-devices list on your Plex account, where you can revoke it at any time. What Plex does with this information is governed by Plex's privacy policy, not this one.

Your own Plex server

Whichever server you sign in to, at whatever address it has. CineDen requests your library listings, cover artwork, and the video itself directly from it, using the token above. On a home network this traffic never leaves your network at all.

Your library contents, what you watch, and how far through it you are all stay between your headset and your server. We are not in that path and cannot see any of it.

What CineDen does not do

These are not settings you have to find and switch off. They are absent from the app.

  • No analytics, usage tracking, or telemetry of any kind.
  • No crash or error reporting sent to us.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No account with us, because we operate no servers or databases.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information, because we hold none.
  • No microphone, camera, or photo access. The app requests none.
  • No collection of your Plex library, viewing history, or watch position.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

Passthrough and your surroundings

CineDen can show your real room behind the screen using the headset's passthrough feature. This is rendered by the headset's own operating system. The app receives no camera images, no depth data, and no information about your surroundings, and it cannot record or transmit any of it.

Removing your data

Because nothing about you is held anywhere but your own headset and your own Plex account, removing it is entirely in your hands.

  • Sign out in CineDen's settings to clear the stored Plex tokens from the headset.
  • Uninstall the app to remove everything in the table above. Android deletes an app's private storage with the app.
  • Revoke access at Plex from the authorised devices list in your Plex account settings, which invalidates the token regardless of what is on the headset.

There is no data export or deletion request to make of us, because we have nothing to export or delete. If you would like that confirmed in writing, write to the address above and we will say so.

Children

CineDen is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children. It plays whatever is in the Plex library you connect it to, so the content it displays is determined by that server and by the Plex Home profile you select. Plex's own parental controls and profile restrictions continue to apply.
